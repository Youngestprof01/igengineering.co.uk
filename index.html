What We Do

Our Services

End-to-end electrical and smart building solutions — from compliance testing to full KNX automation commissioning.

Get a Quote
01
Electrical Installations
New circuits, consumer unit upgrades, distribution boards, and full wiring works for domestic, commercial, and industrial premises. All work fully certificated.
02
Testing & Inspection (EICR)
Periodic inspection and testing to BS 7671 and GN3. Full EICR reports with C1/C2/C3 coded observations for landlords and commercial clients.
03
Commissioning & Fault Finding
Systematic fault diagnosis using IR testing, loop impedance, and continuity checks. Circuit restoration with post-remediation verification.
04
KNX Systems Integration
Installation, ETS6 programming, and commissioning of KNX smart building automation — lighting control, HVAC integration, and sensor configuration.
05
Maintenance & Support
Planned preventative maintenance, remedial works, and ongoing technical support to maintain system integrity and regulatory compliance.
06
Lighting Design (DIALux)
Professional DIALux Evo lighting simulation, lux calculations, and specification reports for new builds and refurbishments.
07
Electrical Design
BS 7671-compliant electrical design for domestic, commercial, and multi-unit residential projects — circuit scheduling, diversity and load calculations, cable sizing, three-phase and single-phase supply design, and distribution board schedules. Full AutoCAD MEP drawings (lighting layouts, panel schedules, annotated circuit plans) delivered alongside DIALux lighting design, with optional KNX/smart-system integration for a fully coordinated electrical package.

Electrical Engineer

Job Type Full-time
Salary £60,000
Hours 37.5 hours per week
Duties & Responsibilities
  • Carrying out the electrical engineering design for a range of commercial and residential buildings.
  • Configuring, installing and commissioning KNX and smart automation systems for energy efficiency, integrating intelligent lighting while maintaining user comfort.
  • Ensuring all jobs are tested, verified and documented according to BS 7671, and maintaining technical documentation.
  • Designing professional lighting using DIALux Evo and developing coordinated electrical drawings and layouts using AutoCAD MEP.
  • Drafting full Electrical Installation Condition Reports for commercial clients and landlords, demonstrating detailed inspection and testing in accordance with BS 7671, and documenting C1/C2/C3 coded observations with the appropriate remedies.
  • Carrying out complete wiring works for domestic, industrial and commercial premises, including new circuit installations, consumer unit upgrades and electrical containment, in accordance with BS 7671 and the specific project specifications.
  • Supervising and monitoring the performance of all electrical installations, building services and smart automation systems to ensure they operate efficiently, reliably and safely.
  • Carrying out initial verification and periodic inspection of domestic, industrial and commercial installations in accordance with both BS 7671 and GN3, identifying defects and potential safety risks.
  • Determining the appropriate installation methods, suitable equipment and materials for the project specifications, and establishing the technical requirements of both electrical and automation systems.
  • Maintaining compliance by ensuring that electrical and building automation systems are designed, tested and installed in accordance with relevant standards and our contractual obligations.
  • Providing clients with post-installation technical support to maintain system integrity and compliance with applicable standards.
  • Designing and configuring control and monitoring systems for electrical and KNX/DALI building automation installations, ensuring that system operation, performance and safety are assessed in accordance with the project specifications and standards.
